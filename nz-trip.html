💡 Notes

  • ⏰ Planned around 9am wake-up / 10am departure; book Milford and the Heli Hike for 10:30-11am. Exception: Day 8, up at 7:30am for Roys Peak
  • 🚗 Drive on the left; winding mountain roads; fill up in Twizel
  • ☀️ Very strong UV: SPF50+ and sunglasses; daylight roughly 6am-9:30pm
  • 🌦️ Helicopter trips depend on weather and may be cancelled at short notice; book changeable tickets
  • 🌙 Day 5 Tekapo: sunset 9:23pm, fully dark 11:47pm, moonrise 0:55am (waning gibbous ~53%) → darkest window ~11:47pm-0:55am
  • 🌙 Day 6 Mt Cook: sunset 9:24pm, fully dark 11:45pm, moonrise 1:17am (~43%) → the NYE countdown falls in the darkest window
  • 🎆 NYE at Mt Cook: very little accommodation in peak season, book first; book the Hermitage Alpine NYE buffet ($165 pp, non-refundable)
  • 🌌 Only one stargazing night (Day 5), no backup; on Day 6 you can stargaze on your own at Mt Cook
  • 📌 At most one big booking per day (Milford, Earnslaw, Heli Hike, stargazing tour); hot pools are optional add-ons after hikes
  • 🧭 Every day in the Daily Plan has an "Open day route in Google Maps" button and a "📍 Download day stops (KML)" button
  • 🚁 The Heli Hike departs Mt Cook Airport (~10 min from the village), about 3h in total; if cancelled, do the Glacier Explorers boat instead
  • 🚁 If Milford is cancelled, move it to Day 3 (Shotover Jet moves to the evening); Glenorchy moves to Day 4, replacing Queenstown Hill
